Meta Information
🚗
Google Car
Left-hand traffic. Distinct Japanese Google car with higher camera setup. Extremely comprehensive coverage including tiny rural roads.
🔌
Utility Poles
One of the densest pole networks in the world. Wooden and concrete poles packed together with countless wires — often looks chaotic and overwhelming.
🚦
Road Signs
Japanese script (kanji, hiragana, katakana) — instantly recognizable. Blue highway signs. Speed limits in km/h. Unique Japanese arrow and warning sign shapes.
🪪
License Plates
Standard: white plate, green border Kei cars: yellow plate, black text Format: prefecture kana + numbers Green plates = standard car
🌍
Language
Japanese — unique script, unmistakable. Mix of kanji (Chinese characters) + hiragana (curved) + katakana (angular). Impossible to mistake for any other country.
🏗️
Architecture
Very distinctive — concrete urban blocks, traditional wooden houses, curved temple roofs. Vending machines everywhere. Very clean and orderly streets.
🌿
Landscape
Mountainous and very green. Rice paddies common in rural areas. Bamboo forests. Coastal areas with dramatic cliffs. Cherry trees (sakura) visible in spring.
⚡
Special Clues
Torii gates (red arch gates) = 100% Japan. Vending machines on roadsides. Unique road markings. White gloves on traffic control workers.
Quick Tips
  • Japanese script is unmistakable — if you see kanji/hiragana/katakana, it's Japan (or possibly Taiwan/China, but those look different)
  • Drive on the left — steering wheel on the right side of cars
  • Vending machines along rural roads = very strongly Japan
  • Torii gates = instantly Japan, no second guess needed
  • Extremely dense utility poles with many wires = Japan (or South Korea, but signs will tell you)
  • Yellow license plates = kei cars, very common and Japan-specific
  • Perfectly maintained roads even in remote rural areas
